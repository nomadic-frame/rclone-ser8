Privacy policy: rclone SER8

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

rclone SER8 is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive only for the owner's account, only to upload, list and manage the owner's backup files.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared or transferred to any third party.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
